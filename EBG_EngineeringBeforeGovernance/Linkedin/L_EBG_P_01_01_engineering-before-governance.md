Your AI policy says every generated change needs human review.

What, exactly, should the human review?

The diff shows what changed. Passing tests show what was checked. Neither establishes which context the agent received or what it was authorized to modify.

That gap appears when broad requirements such as oversight, accountability, and audit reach an individual development decision.
