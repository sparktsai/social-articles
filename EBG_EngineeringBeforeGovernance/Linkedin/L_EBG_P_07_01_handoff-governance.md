That mismatch can make an oversight checkpoint look stronger than the review it actually supports.

Article 07 examines three development handoffs: human to agent, agent to agent, and agent to human.

Each needs a different package. An analysis agent needs bounded instructions and context. A coding agent needs an approved design and explicit authority. A human assessing decision risk needs the supporting impact analysis, controls, and residual uncertainty as well as the code.

The payment retry example shows how to capture the actual transfer, distinguish receipt from readiness, and keep unresolved questions assigned to a named owner.

An agent's role should not silently transfer with its summary. A human's responsibility should match the information and authority they receive.
