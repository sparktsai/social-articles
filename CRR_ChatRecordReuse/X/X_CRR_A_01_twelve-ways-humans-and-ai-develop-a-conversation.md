# Twelve Ways Humans and AI Develop a Conversation

![Twelve forms of conversation between AI and Human](X_CRR_A_01.png)

We often call every interaction with AI a “chat.”

That word preserves the container while hiding the work.

A direct answer, a lesson, a recommendation, a draft, a review finding, and a counterargument do not have the same meaning. They should not be reused in the same way.

The difference comes from what Human and AI are doing together.

Here are twelve useful conversation forms.

## Human Receives Content from AI

### 1. Inquiry

**Human:** questioner  
**AI:** answerer

Inquiry seeks a bounded answer, clarification, command, definition, or fact.

Its record can become a FAQ or operational reference. But an answer is not automatically verified merely because it is direct.

### 2. Learning

**Human:** learner  
**AI:** teacher or explainer

Learning tries to change the Human's mental model.

The reusable material includes explanations, examples, misunderstandings, corrections, and remaining knowledge gaps—not only the final answer.

### 3. Advisory

**Human:** decision-maker  
**AI:** advisor

Advisory compares alternatives under constraints.

Preserve the options, trade-offs, risks, assumptions, and reasons behind the recommendation. AI may recommend. Human owns the decision.

### 4. Assistance

**Human:** primary worker  
**AI:** supporting researcher or checker

Assistance supports another task through lookup, comparison, verification, or local checking.

Its output may be a source lead rather than a result. Record what was checked and what remains uncertain.

## Human and AI Develop Content Together

### 5. Discussion

**Human and AI:** conversation partners

Discussion develops positions around an identifiable topic.

It produces agreements, disagreements, distinctions, and changes in position. Those can become a viewpoint map or the basis for continued discussion.

### 6. Exploration

**Human and AI:** problem explorers

Exploration may begin before the problem is clearly defined.

It produces hypotheses, branches, new questions, and unexpected connections. Some branches can become separate Topics or research tasks.

### 7. Analysis

**Human:** defines the problem and conditions  
**AI:** analyst

Analysis develops a judgment from criteria, evidence, assumptions, and alternatives.

Its value is not only the conclusion. Preserve the basis so the judgment can be revisited when conditions change.

### 8. Creation

**Human:** initiator and direction owner  
**AI:** co-creator or producer

Creation develops an artifact such as an article, design, specification, presentation, or code.

The record may contain rejected titles, unused structures, draft fragments, and revision directions that remain valuable after the final artifact is complete.

## AI Evaluates or Challenges Human Work

### 9. Critique

**Human:** presents an idea or artifact  
**AI:** critic

Critique evaluates clarity, coherence, strength, audience fit, and weakness.

It can become an improvement plan. Its criteria may be interpretive rather than formal requirements.

### 10. Review

**Human:** submits and decides how to correct  
**AI:** inspector

Review checks an artifact against rules, requirements, or acceptance conditions.

It should produce traceable findings, affected areas, expected corrections, and status.

Critique asks how well something works. Review asks whether it meets defined expectations.

### 11. Dialectic

**Human:** claimant or questioner  
**AI:** challenger

Dialectic tests a claim using counterarguments and counterexamples.

Its reusable value lies in the exchange: original claim, challenge, response, failure condition, and revised claim.

## AI Helps Human Look Back

### 12. Reflection

**Human:** reviews experience and thinking  
**AI:** reflection partner

Reflection examines what the Human believed, what changed, and what remains unresolved.

It can become a retrospective or learning note. It is a record of Human reflection—not an AI verdict about what the Human “really thinks.”

## One Chat Can Contain Several Forms

A conversation can move through:

```text
Inquiry → Discussion → Exploration → Analysis
→ Creation → Critique → Review → Reflection
```

The forms are not mutually exclusive labels for an entire session.

Verification, revision, organization, and simulation are often activities inside a form rather than separate Human–AI relationships.

For example:

```text
Primary form: Assistance
Activity: Verification

Primary form: Creation
Activity: Revision
```

## Similar Forms Still Need Different Treatment

Several forms can look alike in a transcript.

Inquiry and Learning both include questions. Inquiry is complete when the bounded answer is obtained. Learning is complete only when the Human's understanding changes. A learning record therefore needs examples, misunderstandings, and corrections.

Advisory and Assistance both help the Human. Advisory helps choose among options. Assistance supports work whose direction the Human already owns. A source lead from Assistance should not quietly become a recommendation.

Discussion and Exploration both branch. Discussion develops positions around a known subject. Exploration may still be finding the useful problem. Preserve disagreements from the first and possible directions from the second.

Analysis and Creation can also blur when AI turns reasoning into a polished proposal. If the intended mode was Analysis, preserve the criteria and assumptions before the artifact hides them.

Critique, Review, and Dialectic all evaluate something, but differently:

- Critique asks how well it works.
- Review asks whether it meets defined expectations.
- Dialectic asks when the claim fails.

These distinctions determine whether the next artifact should be an improvement plan, traceable finding, or revised argument.

## One Project Can Use All Twelve

Imagine a team deciding whether to add AI review to a publishing workflow.

Inquiry clarifies the meaning of critique and review. Learning builds a shared mental model. Advisory compares workflows. Assistance gathers platform rules. Discussion surfaces editorial positions. Exploration discovers that provenance is a separate problem.

Analysis compares alternatives against cost and risk. Creation produces the operating guide. Critique checks clarity. Review checks requirements. Dialectic challenges the claim that the new step always improves quality. Reflection captures what the team learns after adoption.

This is one Topic, but the content does not have one status.

A brainstorm is not a commitment. A recommendation is not approval. A draft is not a verified specification. A reflection is not an audit finding.

## Why the Form Matters

Each form suggests a different reuse path:

```text
Inquiry   → FAQ
Learning  → study notes
Advisory  → decision record
Assistance → verification trail
Discussion → viewpoint map
Exploration → research questions
Analysis  → comparison or model
Creation  → formal artifact
Critique  → improvement plan
Review    → issues and status
Dialectic → stronger claim
Reflection → retrospective
```

Topic Chat Recorder can preserve selected Human–AI messages with session metadata in portable Markdown.

It does not automatically classify these forms, verify AI answers, build a knowledge base, or prove authorship.

Those remain Human judgments.

The same AI output can mean different things because Human and AI were doing different things together.

Before reusing the words, preserve the relationship that produced them.
