Your AI conversation has been exported to Markdown.

Why is it still not reusable knowledge?

Because recording is only the first step.

This content series began with a simple AI conversation: What kinds of conversations do people have with AI?

The first answer was only a list. Then we questioned the classification basis, reframed the types around Human–AI roles, clarified Assistance as lookup and verification support, connected each form to reuse paths, and separated the recorder from downstream tools.

The recorded Topic eventually supported a twelve-form interaction model, product details, Medium stories, LinkedIn Articles and Posts, and plans for X.

That transformation followed six stages:

**1. Record**

Preserve the part of the conversation that belongs to the Topic.

**2. Select**

Identify questions, assumptions, options, decisions, findings, examples, and unfinished work.

**3. Verify**

Check facts, numbers, citations, product behavior, and external claims.

**4. Organize**

Separate Topics, assign status, connect related material, and choose the downstream system.

**5. Transform**

Create the appropriate note, matrix, article, issue, procedure, or decision record.

**6. Trace**

Keep a practical path between the output and the conversation that shaped it.

```text
Record → Select → Verify → Organize → Transform → Trace
```

The recorder should not pretend to perform all six stages. Its role is smaller: preserve selected Human–AI interaction as portable input.

Obsidian may organize notes. Zotero may manage sources. A spreadsheet may structure a decision. An issue tracker may manage findings. Git may version the resulting artifact.

Recording preserves possibility. Selection, verification, organization, and transformation realize the value.
