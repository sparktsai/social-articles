Why does every team have to rebuild AI Rules from engineering knowledge that already exists?

Style guides and quality practices contain useful constraints. Yet that knowledge often stays in human-facing documents or project-specific instructions, making reuse across teams difficult.

If you were facing this, what would you do?

This article explores a thought experiment: shared Rule Libraries that teams can discover, adapt, and combine for their own contexts.
