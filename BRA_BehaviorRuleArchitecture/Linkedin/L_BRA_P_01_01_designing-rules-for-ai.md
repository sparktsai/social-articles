Why do you have to repeat the same AI instructions every time a new task starts?

You add another constraint after each mistake. The prompt grows, yet the next session still needs the same reminders. Moving them into comments or project files creates another place to maintain them.

If you were facing this, what would you do?

In this article, I trace how repeated instructions became reusable Rules and Rulesets, and why that led me to Behavior Rule Architecture.
