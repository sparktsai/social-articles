# First Relay: What If the Evidence Is Correct, but the Specification Is Wrong?

In my original post, I asked what evidence a human would need before merging AI-generated code they could not read.

But there is an earlier hidden gap.

Vibe Coding begins with human intent. Specification-Driven Development begins with a specification.

Who verifies the transition between them?

The specification, code, tests, and evidence may all agree with one another—and still represent the wrong interpretation of what the human wanted.

This risk becomes greater when the same AI generates the specification, implementation, tests, and final evidence. The entire chain may be internally consistent without being aligned with the original intent.

A green test report can show that the implementation satisfies the specification. It cannot, by itself, show that the specification captured the right intent.

Even Human-readable Evidence may accurately explain the wrong implementation if the misunderstanding began earlier.

**Before an AI-generated specification becomes the source of truth, what must a human verify?**
