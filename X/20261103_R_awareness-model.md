Suppose a merged AI retained recognizable traces of two models. That would tell us something about learned structure, but would it tell us anything about minds?

Now extend the thought experiment to a person. If a system preserved your memories, preferences, habits, and way of reasoning, would it preserve you—or only reproduce your behavior?

Recognition, continuity, and experience are different tests. People might recognize the responses while the question of first-person continuity remains unanswered.

If everything observable about you survived, what would still need to continue for you to call it the same person?
