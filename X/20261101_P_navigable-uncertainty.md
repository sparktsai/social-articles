# Making Uncertainty Navigable

## Main Post

AI is often designed to collapse uncertainty into an answer. Perhaps its more valuable role is to make uncertainty navigable—not to remove it.

## Relay 1 — Reply from the main account

Preserve alternatives. Expose branching points. Record the conditions behind a choice.

## Relay 2 — Reply to Relay 1

When conditions change, the map should change too. Every intermediate node can become a new starting point.

## Relay 3 — Reply to Relay 2

What kind of problem would you most want to explore this way?

## Optional Relay — Reply from a related second account

Making uncertainty visible is useful only if people can act on it. A map still needs decision points, ownership, and a rule for when exploration ends.
