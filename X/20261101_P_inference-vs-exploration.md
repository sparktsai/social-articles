# Inference vs. Exploration

## Main Post

Most AI use follows a familiar pattern: analysis → answer, conditions → decision, content → critique. But all assume the problem already has a defined range. What if it does not?

## Relay 1 — Reply from the main account

Inference works within a known range. Exploration helps reveal what the range could be.

## Relay 2 — Reply to Relay 1

The output is not only an answer. It is a map of possible paths, conditions, alternatives, and probabilities.

## Relay 3 — Reply to Relay 2

When the destination is uncertain, should AI still try to converge?

## Optional Relay — Reply from a related second account

One tension: exploration can expand forever. The harder problem may be deciding when to keep branching, when to update the range, and when to stop.
